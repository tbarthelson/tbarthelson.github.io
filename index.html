The Camping Window

When relying on a tent in the Tasmanian alpine, timing is survival. February is your safest bet for dry ground and manageable nights, though snow is possible year-round.

  • ✓ Best Time: Late Jan to Early March.
  • ⚠ Gear Check: 4-Season tent & -5°C sleeping bag recommended for Walls of Jerusalem.
