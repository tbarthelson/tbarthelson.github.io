Logistics & Safety

Critical information for a safe January departure. Check these items off as you prepare.

🔥

Summer Risks (Jan)

  • Fire Danger: Download "Fires Near Me" app. Do not hike on Catastrophic rating days.
  • Extreme UV: SPF 50+ is mandatory. The ozone hole effect is real.
  • Wildlife on Roads: Avoid driving at dawn/dusk to protect wallabies and devils.
🎫

Mandatory Bookings

  • National Parks Pass: Buy "Holiday Pass" online (covers vehicle + 8 weeks).
  • Spirit of Tasmania: Vehicle slot booked (Return).
  • Cradle Mtn Shuttle: Included in pass, but verify access times (cars banned at Dove Lake).
